04 · Origin

      

        

          
One word runs through everything I build: clarity.

          

            

              I came to design from engineering. Early on, I watched someone struggle with a tool I had built and was proud of,
              and it landed hard: what was effortless for me, the maker, was a wall for them, the user. That flipped my question
              from what the technology can do to who is using it, and why. Twenty-five years later the job is the same. Find the
              real problem, not the symptom, and fix it.
            

            

              I never left engineering behind. I design in code, so what ships is what I designed, with nothing lost in a handoff.
              At Walmart I built the Data Ventures design system from scratch on the company's own design language. At
              Apple I lead design for the AI-native platforms behind Apple Pay, and I build design systems in code that
              extend Apple's own.
            

            

              I lead as a player-coach. I set direction by building alongside the team, raise the craft bar in the open, and try
              to scale judgment rather than hoard it. The systems I am proudest of kept growing after I stepped back, because the
              people using them could extend them themselves.
