Apple · Wallet, Payments & Commerce

            
I let the AI suggest, never silently act.

            

              Internal logistics ran on manual workflows. The temptation was full automation.
              I made every AI action named, explainable, and reversible, so managers stayed
              in control. Trust was the design problem, not speed.

04 Origin

      

      

        

          

            I started as a software engineer. That decision shaped everything.
          

          

            

              Most designers hand off to engineering. I sit at the seam, where a component-architecture
              choice decides whether a team moves fast or grinds to a halt. That's where I've spent 25 years.
            

            

              At Walmart I architected the DV Design System and the component foundation for
              Luminate, where merchants make multi-million-dollar inventory calls. At Apple's
              Wallet, Payments & Commerce team I drive an AI-augmented workflow that takes a
              feature from brief to production-ready React in under two weeks, at Apple-standard quality.
            

            

              I don't design pictures of software. I design software, and then I ship it.
