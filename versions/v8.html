B1How I WorkThe Method

Same four moves, every time.

i

Find the real problem

Research. Ask why until it hurts. Watch what people do, not only what they say.

ii

Make it clear

Systems thinking. Reduce cognitive load. Design the order the eye travels: status first, detail last.

iii

Prove it

Prototype. Test with real users. Tie the result to a business number that matters.

iv

Lead it

Player-coach. Scale judgment, not just output. The best mentoring makes you unnecessary.

And I build in code, so what ships is what I designed, not a diluted copy of it.

B2OriginTwenty-five years

I came to design from engineering. Early on I watched someone struggle with a tool I had built and was proud of, and it landed hard: what was effortless for me, the maker, was a wall for them, the user. That flipped my question from what the technology can do to who is using it, and why. Twenty-five years later the job is the same: find the real problem, not the symptom, and fix it. I lead as a player-coach, and the systems I am proudest of kept growing after I stepped back.
