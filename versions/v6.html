01 The spine

Clarity is the whole job.

They say “We need a dashboard.”
They say “Add AI to it.”

Stakeholders hand me a solution disguised as a problem. My job is to find the real need, the one they cannot quite name, and make it clear. Whether the surface is a design system, an analytics platform, or an AI agent, the work is the same: find the real problem, not the symptom, and solve it.

03 How I work

Find it. Clarify it. Prove it. Lead it.

01

Find the real problem

Research, ask why, and watch what people actually do. The brief is rarely the problem.

02

Make it clear

Systems thinking. Reduce cognitive load. Design the order the eye travels: status first, detail last.

03

Prove it

Prototype and test with real users, then tie the outcome to a business number. I build in code, so what ships is what I designed.

04

Lead it

Player-coach. Scale judgment across teams. The best mentoring makes you unnecessary.

04 Origin

Who is using it, and why.

I came to design from engineering. Early on I watched someone struggle with a tool I had built and was proud of, and it landed hard: what was effortless for me, the maker, was a wall for them, the user.

That flipped my question from what the technology can do to who is using it, and why. Twenty-five years later the job is the same: find the real problem, not the symptom, and fix it. I lead as a player-coach, and the systems I am proudest of kept growing after I stepped back.
